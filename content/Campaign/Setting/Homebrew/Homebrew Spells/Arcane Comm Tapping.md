# Arcane Comm Tapping

_4th-Level Divination_

**Classes:** Wizard, Warlock  
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** V, S, M (a small tuning fork made of silver, worth 250 gp)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

## Description:

You reach into the threads of the Weave to intercept magical communication within range. Choose a magical communication channel you are aware of, such as sending stones, a telepathic link, or a spell like _Sending_. The caster or originator of the communication must make a saving throw using their spell save DC.

- On a critical success, the communication proceeds as normal, and the participants are made aware of the attempt.
- On a success, the communication proceeds as normal, and you cannot intercept it.
- On a failure, you gain access to the communication for the duration of the spell, allowing you to hear, see, or otherwise perceive the magical exchange as if you were a participant.
- On a critical failure, you gain access to the communication and can choose to disrupt, alter, or block the message, causing confusion or misdirection.
