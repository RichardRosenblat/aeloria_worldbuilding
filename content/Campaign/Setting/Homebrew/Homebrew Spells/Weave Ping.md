# Weave Ping

"Where are you hiding little pig?" -Thabrys, The Unseen Lich, seaching for interlopers in his domain.

_7rd-Level Divination_

**Classes:** Wizard, Sorcerer, Druid  
**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** Touch  
**Components:** V, S, M (a shard of quartz and a pinch of powdered iron)  
**Duration:** Concentration, up to 10 minutes

## Description:

You attune your senses to the Weave, focusing on a magical item, effect, or creature you can touch. Upon casting, only you see lines of magical energy connecting the target to other magical auras or sources within 60 feet of you. Highlighting creatures, magical items, or effects that are within range. Creatures, items or effects that are deeply connected to the target are highlighted with a brighter, more intense aura.

To discern specific information or locate connections, you must make an Intelligence (Arcana) check against a DC set by the DM, depending on the strength or subtlety of the connections (typically DC 15 for simple auras or DC 20+ for hidden or ancient links).


## At Higher Levels:

When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 8th level or higher, the duration increases 1 hour and the range of the spell increases 10 feet for each slot level above 7th.
