# Spell Hijack

"No the fuck you don't!" - Vivian Thorett, The 13th Archmage of the Stenklif Tower clan

*4th-Level Abjuration*

**Classes:** Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock , Bard
**Casting Time:** 1 reaction, taken when a creature you can see within 60 feet casts a spell  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous  

## Description:  
You attempt to wrest control of a spell as it is being cast by another creature within range. As part of this reaction, make a contested spellcasting ability check against the original caster's spellcasting ability check.  

- On a success, you redirect the spell's target to a new target within the spell's range. If the spell affects an area, you can reposition the area within the original spell's range.  
- On a failure, the spell proceeds as intended, and you take psychic damage equal to twice the spell’s level.  

You cannot hijack a spell with a casting time longer than 1 action or reaction, nor can you redirect spells with a range of "Self."  
You can only hijack spells of 4th level or lower.

## At Higher Levels:  
When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 5th level or higher, you gain advantage on the contested spellcasting ability check. Additionally, you can redirect spells of one level higher for each slot above 4th.
