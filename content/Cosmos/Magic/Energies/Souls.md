# Souls in [[Aeloria]]

Souls are divine creations, brought into existence by the two Overdeities of [[Aeloria]]: **[[Aena, goddess of kindness, creation and order - The Architect (LG)|Aena,the Architect]]**, and **[[Kryela, goddess of malice, destruction and chaos - The Undoing (CE)|Kryela, the Undoing]]**. These ethereal entities are the essence of life, animating the physical forms of living beings and guiding them through their journey in the world. 

## Creation and Birth of Souls

Souls are crafted by [[Aena, goddess of kindness, creation and order - The Architect (LG)|Aena,the Architect]], from beyond [[the Greater Limit]] — a barrier that separates the immense power of the Overdeities from creation itself. Each year during the summer solstice, these souls are sent into the [[Astral Plane]] through [[the Greater Limit]].

There, [[Pelor, God of the Sun, Summer and Agriculture - The Dawnfather (NG)]], guides the souls to the heart of the [[Astral Plane]]. This is where they await their eventual birth into the mortal realm.

## The Cycle of a Soul

1. **Life and Growth**: Once born, a soul grows and develops alongside its physical host, shaped by its experiences, emotions, and lessons.
2. **Death and Afterlife**: After death, the soul travels to the [[Metapsychic Planes|Outer Planes]] and becomes a petitioner in the afterlife. While there, it faces different fates based on its actions and beliefs.
3. **Final Death**: If a petitioner’s soul is destroyed or consumed in the [[Metapsychic Planes|Outer Planes]], it leaves behind a faint, powerless husk — a trace of its existence.
4. **Return to [[the Greater Limit]]**: These husks are collected by The [[Raven Queen, Goddess of Death, Crows and Winter - The Matron of Death (LN)]]. During the winter solstice, she releases these remnants through [[the Greater Limit]], where they are ultimately taken by [[Kryela, goddess of malice, destruction and chaos - The Undoing (CE)|Kryela, the Undoing]], for their final end.

## Nature of Souls

Souls are composed of **pure meta-elemental energy**, an energy that serves as the base for all other forms of elemental energy in the universe. As such, they are a form of existence that only [[Aena, goddess of kindness, creation and order - The Architect (LG)|the Architect]] can create and [[Kryela, goddess of malice, destruction and chaos - The Undoing (CE)|the Undoing]] can completely destroy. While the Overdeities hold ultimate dominion over souls, other [[true gods]], creatures, spells, and artifacts can interact with them in various ways:

- **Manipulation**: Souls can be manipulated, altered, or transformed by divine beings, powerful artifacts, or spells. Changes to a soul can have profound effects on the individual it animates.
- **Damage**: Creatures of the [[Metapsychic Planes|Outer Planes]] can harm souls by consuming, tearing apart, purifying, or corrupting them. Such damage can have lasting consequences for the soul and its host.
- **Division**: Souls can be divided into fragments, each containing a portion of the original soul’s essence. These fragments can be separated, merged, or destroyed, affecting the individual’s personality and identity.
- **Consumption**: Some entities can consume souls to gain power, knowledge, or sustenance. Consumed souls are usually destroyed, leaving behind only a trace of their existence.

## The Seed of Existence

Souls function like seeds, containing a unique sequence of numbers that determine their potential. These numbers interact with inputs — experiences, lessons, emotions, and more — to generate outputs, which manifest as the personality and individuality of a being.

### Key Properties of Souls
- A soul, if unchanged or undamaged, will always produce the same output given the same input.
- Two different souls will never produce identical outputs, even if given the same input.
- The outputs of a soul are known as **spirits**, which animate the physical forms of living beings. But both terms are often used interchangeably, as a soul is the source of a spirit and a spirit is an iteration of a soul.

This intricate design ensures that each soul is unique, contributing to the vast tapestry of life and existence in [[Aeloria]].
